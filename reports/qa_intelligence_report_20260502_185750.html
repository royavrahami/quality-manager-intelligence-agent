By integrating the Grey Wolf Optimizer (GWO) mechanism, the framework selects leader agents to guide the optimization process, aiming for stable and robust reasoning configurations. Experimental results demonstrate that Agent-GWO enhances both accuracy and stability across various reasoning benchmarks compared to existing methods.
