Agent-GWO: Collaborative Agents for Dynamic Prompt Optimization in Large Language Models
🕵️ AI Agents 14 Apr 2026 07:35 UTC
Summary
The paper introduces Agent-GWO, a novel framework for dynamic prompt optimization in Large Language Models (LLMs). It addresses the limitations of current methods that rely on static prompts and single-agent local searches by using a collaborative agent approach based on the Grey Wolf Optimizer (GWO). This method unifies prompt templates and decoding hyperparameters, enabling more stable and accurate reasoning across various benchmarks. The framework has shown improved performance in mathematical and hybrid reasoning tasks.
Key Insights
▸ Agent-GWO uses a leader-follower mechanism to optimize both prompts and decoding parameters, enhancing reasoning stability.
▸ The framework integrates prompt templates and hyperparameters into agent configurations, allowing for iterative improvements.
▸ Extensive testing shows that Agent-GWO outperforms existing methods in terms of accuracy and stability across different LLM backbones.
💼 Why This Matters for Quality Managers
For a QA Manager or Tech Project Manager, understanding Agent-GWO is crucial as it offers a more reliable method for optimizing LLM performance, which can directly impact the quality and consistency of AI-driven applications. This framework's ability to enhance reasoning accuracy and stability can lead to more robust testing and validation processes, ensuring higher software quality and more predictable project outcomes.
